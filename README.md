### 🔬 About Me

I'm an AI master's student at **Iran University of Science and Technology (IUST)**. My research curiosity lives at the crossroads of:

- 🧬 **AI × Bioinformatics & Neuroscience** — using AI to make sense of biological and neural signals
- 🔍 **Explainable & Trustworthy AI** — models that can show their work, not just their output
- 🖇️ **Multi-Modal Learning** — fusing signals across vision, language, and other modalities
- 👁️ **Computer Vision**
- 📝 **Natural Language Processing**

I'm especially drawn to problems where the *"why"* behind a model's decision matters as much as the decision itself.

### 🚀 Featured Projects

**🧬 Medical Imaging**

- **[Breast Tumor Segmentation](https://github.com/AidaKhaleghi/Breast_Tumor_Segmentation)** — A multi-task MONAI pipeline for breast ultrasound that jointly performs tumor segmentation and benign/malignant classification through a shared UNet encoder.

**🧠 Generative & Representation Learning**

- **[VAE on MNIST](https://github.com/AidaKhaleghi/VAE_MNIST)** — A convolutional variational autoencoder built from scratch in PyTorch, with latent-space interpolation and a full 2D latent-space sweep to visualize how digit classes are organized.

**👁️ Computer Vision**

- **[MVAM: Multi-View Steering Angle Prediction](https://github.com/AidaKhaleghi/MVAM_Steering_Angle)** — Predicts a car's steering angle from three dashboard camera views using a shared ResMLP backbone with multi-head attention for view fusion.
- **[YOLOv11 Segmentation in Adverse Weather (CARLA)](https://github.com/AidaKhaleghi/YOLOv11_Carla_Segmentation)** — Benchmarks YOLOv11 instance segmentation for autonomous driving under fog, rain, and night conditions, with a custom CARLA data-generation pipeline, automated GroundedSAM labeling, and a ~4× inference speedup via TensorRT. 
- **[Multi-Scale Featured LBP for Facial Expression Recognition](https://github.com/AidaKhaleghi/Multi-Scale-Featured-Local-Binary-Pattern)** — Implementation of a published method proposing a robust multi-scale Local Binary Pattern descriptor for facial expression recognition.
- **[Iranian License Plate Tamper Detection](https://github.com/AidaKhaleghi/Plate_Tamper_Detection)** — A classical (non-deep-learning) OpenCV pipeline that localizes, verifies, and scores license plates for signs of tampering or obscuring.

**🤖 LLMs, Agents & NLP**

- **[Multi-Agent HPO](https://github.com/AidaKhaleghi/Multi-agent-HPO)** — Collaborating on a multi-agent approach to hyperparameter optimization.
- **[Multi-Agent Debate System](https://github.com/AidaKhaleghi/Multi-Agent_Debate_System)** — A LangGraph-based simulation where two LLM agents argue opposite sides of a topic and a judge panel scores the outcome, comparing single-model vs. multi-model judging setups.
- **[Plan-and-Solve Prompting Reproduction](https://github.com/AidaKhaleghi/Plan_and_Solve_Reproduction)** — Reproduces the *Plan-and-Solve* zero-shot prompting paper across four reasoning benchmarks using LLaMA 3.3, benchmarked against the original GPT-3 results.
